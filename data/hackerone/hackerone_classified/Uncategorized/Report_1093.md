# HackerOne Report: LinkedIN URL should be HTTPS
**Report ID:** 1093
**Vulnerability Class:** Uncategorized

## Vulnerability Information & PoC
Not really a security bug, but I think will be a good idea to add HTTPS on LinkedIN Share Button.

Example page (In the right side of page):
https://hackerone.com/reports/547

LinkedIN redirect to HTTPS after click, but cookie is sent on the network before that.
