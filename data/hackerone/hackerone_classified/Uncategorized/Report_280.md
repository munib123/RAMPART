# HackerOne Report: Real impersonation
**Report ID:** 280
**Vulnerability Class:** Uncategorized

## Vulnerability Information & PoC
When you view [merijn.html](https://hackerone.com/merijn.html) his profile shows the profile page of the [merijn](https://hackerone.com/merijn) user although the former is a completely different user. This way users can potentially impersonate other users.
